# `rod_sort_lib`

Rod sort over a branching strategy and a rod merge. Hosts the BranchingStrategy trait, its six strategies and the merges, three of its own and one over the tree sort family's trees, since rod sort is their only consumer, and `RodGaps`, a strategy's gap chain as a Shell sort gap sequence.

Its catalog components are declared in `rod.spec`.

Query `rod.avbs` builds: `RodSorts`.
